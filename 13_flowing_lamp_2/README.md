# 1.3 Flowing water lamp experiment
Use the following method to realize the D1-D8 lights,

Use the table lookup method to realize the four kinds of flow lights of D1-D8, 
such as cycling from left to right, cycling from right to left, cycling from left to right, and cycling from both 
sides to the middle, and cycling from the middle to both sides, with an interval of 100ms. Pay attention to 
the continuity of the lights, and there must be no gaps.
